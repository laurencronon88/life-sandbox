WHY THESE MATERIALS / ONE SHARED FOUNDATION

Three looks.
One five-star feeling.

We're selecting a direction, not locking ourselves into a matching set. Every layer can be mixed and matched across the three concepts.

01 / PERCALE

Cool by design.

We chose cotton percale because its crisp, matte plain weave feels like a freshly made luxury bed and breathes well. Its relatively flat surface is also generally more sensible around cat claws than loose or textured weaves—though no fabric is claw-proof.

02 / TEXTURE

Layered, not heavy.

Linen is one option for relaxed depth, not a requirement. Silk pillowcases deliver the smooth face-contact layer we wanted; an optional light wool insert provides adaptable warmth. Percale can carry the entire duvet for a more cat-conscious alternative.

03 / FLEXIBILITY

Mix everything.

Our non-negotiables stay identical across all three: cooling sheets, silk pillowcases, and a weighted blanket for Lauren alone. White and ivory allow easy mixing. Choose a linen or percale duvet independently of the other components; Matt's side stays unweighted.

Room images are illustrative styling studies, not exact product photography. Knitted throws are visual inspiration and are not in the totals. In a cat household, avoid loosely knitted top layers and place a smooth washable cover over the cats' favorite kneading spot. Neither linen nor percale is tear-proof.
