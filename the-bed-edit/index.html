03 / THE SHARED SYSTEM

Better together.
Comfort individually.

The comfort foundation remains the same. The aesthetic—and how we arrange the layers—changes.

01 / BOTH

Percale + silk

Crisp, breathable cotton percale gives a cool, matte foundation. Two silk pillowcases soften contact; no fabric guarantees a particular sleep temperature.

02 / EACH

Our own pillow

Lauren: adjustable Nest Easy Breather. Matt: adjustable Coop Cool+. Both fit the selected 20 × 28-inch silk cases, with slightly different fits.

03 / TOGETHER

Layers that adapt

One light wool insert plus one cotton quilt, arranged differently all year. Lauren's weighted blanket stays on her side only; Matt never needs to sleep under it.

04 / OUR CATS

Linen is a choice

Only Soft & Textured uses linen. It brings beautiful character but may snag on claws. Both cotton-only directions are more practical, though no fabric is claw-proof.
