02 / Styling guide

Three looks, same products

01 / Without quilt

Let the Ivory Quince duvet cover and Woolroom insert carry the bed. Fold the duvet top down for fullness. Use the two Quince standard shams with your silk-covered sleeping pillows; no Euro shams. The Bearaby can be stored.

02 / Quilt fully spread

Spread the Peacock Alley Mia across the bed, with the ivory duvet turned back near the pillows. Leave the chunky weighted blanket off for a calmer look.

03 / Quilt folded

Fold the Mia neatly across the lower third of the ivory duvet. Add the purchased Driftwood Bearaby as a compact centered fold only when wanted, avoiding two bulky foot-of-bed layers.

The separate 4-page PDF remains the visual mood-board reference. This web page is the purchase-status source of truth.

04 / Before buying

What needs checking

Mia swatches: Peacock Alley lists the Mia coverlet and shams as final sale. Compare real Pearl and Dune samples before committing.

Rug: Both cats scratch rugs. Nothing here is scratch-proof; prioritize the low-profile option and confirm 8 × 10 ft clearance.

Insert fit: The Woolroom Queen/Full insert is 86 × 86 in. Verify the exact Quince Full/Queen cover dimensions and how it fits after laundering.

Bearaby: Ocean Napper in Driftwood, 15 lb, is purchased. No additional weighted blanket is needed.
